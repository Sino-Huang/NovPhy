# Manuscript Research Evidence Ledger

## Scope and Research Target

**NovPhy benchmark.** NovPhy is the published physical-reasoning benchmark for open-world AI systems. The published paper evaluates novelty detection and adaptation across five physical scenarios and eight novelty levels. The canonical citation is V. Pinto, C. Gamage, C. Xue, P. Zhang, E. Nikonova, M. Stephenson, and J. Renz, “NovPhy: A physical reasoning benchmark for open-world AI systems,” *Artificial Intelligence*, vol. 336, p. 104198, 2024. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.artint.2024.104198. It is distinct from the BG-NS-JEPA work described here.

**BG-NS-JEPA program.** BG-NS-JEPA is an in-progress world-model program for action-sparse persistent-effect environments. Its controller selects a requested horizon and description mode, while a continuous latent carries the rollout. The central method-advantage claim is resolved as a bounded negative: the authorized issue-15 confirmatory returned `not_supported_by_this_experiment` at both frozen compute-support budgets. The manuscript was reframed on 2026-09-21 as a boundary-anatomy study and, after the #82–#92 wave, re-centred (2026-09-23, issue #91) on the **measured ranking failure** (engine-truth ceiling 7/14 members; frozen rankers 0/108 top-1 on N1, scoped by the 42/1,224 counterexample). Binding plan: [writing_outline_boundary_paper.md](writing_outline_boundary_paper.md) v2.0.

**Central research question.** Does the joint-pair controller improve a common endpoint against `fixed-pair`, `temporal-only`, `description-only`, `independent-axes`, and the parameter-matched two-head controller? **Executed and answered in the bounded negative** under the authorized frozen protocol (issue 15, seed-4505, six complete sealed rollouts): zero paired gain against the parameter-matched two-head controller at the lower budget; paired gain $-5.2\times10^{-4}$, 95% interval $[-7.1\times10^{-4},-3.6\times10^{-4}]$, against fixed-pair at the higher budget; no increase in authoritative endpoint physical-violation incidence; disposition `not_supported_by_this_experiment` within instance-held-out same-physics scope.
