\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:rel}

\paragraph{Decision-relevant prediction.} Objective-mismatch studies show that aggregate predictive quality need not equal decision quality: one-step prediction likelihood need not track control performance, and higher pixel accuracy need not yield better physical-reasoning performance on PHYRE \citep{lambert2020objective,girdhar2020forward}. We add an engine-truth per-candidate AUC under a measured per-state ceiling in a single-shot cascade regime.

\paragraph{Closed-loop evaluation.} World-in-World benchmarks world models inside a closed loop with a unified online planning strategy and a standardized action interface, with task success as the primary metric, and reports that visual quality alone does not guarantee task success \citep{zhang2026worldinworld}. WorldBench evaluates video world models with physics concepts isolated one at a time \citep{upadhyay2026worldbench}. Our measurement adds a per-state action ceiling measured in the engine as the denominator of a closed-loop success rate (Section~\ref{sec:syn:picture}). PHYRE defines tier membership by solvability within a fixed action set, operationalized via a sampled-action binomial test, and scores multi-attempt task success by AUCCESS \citep{bakhtin2019phyre}; World-in-World instead reports task success without that task-set solvability label \citep{zhang2026worldinworld}.
