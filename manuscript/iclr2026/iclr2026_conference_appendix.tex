\section{Reproducibility Statement}

\begin{revblock}
Section~\ref{sec:mth:protocol} defines the splits, metrics, statistics and live protocol. Appendix~\ref{sec:app:protocol} records the split construction, inventories, scoring path and guards, and Appendix~\ref{sec:app:method} gives every architectural and training setting of the primary model and the pixel-study models. Appendix~\ref{sec:app:repro} lists the checkpoints, the validation command of each artifact family and the artifact behind every printed number. In the recorded reproduction check dated 2026-10-23, 23 of 32 commands exited successfully; nine stopped at frozen-input integrity checks before recomputation. The reproduction appendix reports each artifact's last observed validation status and date. No experiment validator was rerun for this manuscript revision. Validations of later experiment programs are listed there separately with their own status and date. The supplement contains the submitted-era development ranking cells and candidate verdicts, their recorded aggregate-reproduction checks, and exact split membership for the CLEVRER and Physion pixel studies. Per-level ranking cells, per-episode live outcomes, and level-by-candidate verdicts for the sealed NovPhy evaluation, including the submitted-checkpoint rerun, are not included. The supplement also lists the 1,000 sealed NovPhy levels with their predictor-fit, policy-fit or evaluation role as recorded in the retained run records, together with per-arm live outcome counts, the typed live failures and the full AUC--MAC table. The frozen roster file itself is not included, nor are the per-inventory capture lists of the 120 evaluation levels with non-grid inventories.
\end{revblock}

\section{Ethics Statement}
\rev{This work uses simulated physics only: NovPhy levels \citep{pinto2024novphy}, CLEVRER videos and annotations \citep{yi2020clevrer}, released under a CC0 license, and Physion-Dominoes stimuli \citep{bear2021physion}, released under the MIT license.} It involves no human subjects, personal data, or deployed systems.
