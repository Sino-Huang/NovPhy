\section{Setting and Formulation}
\label{sec:mth:setting}

An \emph{action-sparse persistent-effect environment} proceeds in discrete fixed steps. An agent acts at a few steps, and the state then evolves through collisions before settling. We instantiate this class on NovPhy \citep{pinto2024novphy}. BG-NS-JEPA, the in-progress program studied here, supplies the models and every number we report. Its scenario families serve as comparison strata: the original single-force and multiple-forces families, the rolling and sliding families added for breadth (N1), and matched appearance-novelty pairs (N2). One out-of-family replication uses CLEVRER \citep{yi2020clevrer} (Appendix~\ref{sec:app:clevrer:results}).

Each decision state receives a prediction request with a requested horizon $\Delta \in \{1, 5, 15\}$ (written $h$ later) and an output-description mode $\alpha \in \{\mathrm{continuous}, \mathrm{micro}, \mathrm{macro}\}$, selecting the scored readout: the predictive latent, fine contact and support relations, or coarse predicates such as \texttt{steady-state}. All nine pairs are readouts over one continuous latent carrier. A fixed-horizon ranker applies its $\Delta$-conditioned carrier $\Delta$ times per candidate, each advancing $\Delta$ carrier frames (of 50 native steps), so it looks $\Delta^2$ carrier frames ahead (1, 25, 225), while engine-truth outcomes resolve 97.8--400.9 carrier frames after the decision (Appendix~\ref{sec:app:lookahead}).

Each development N1 decision state carries the original 13-candidate sweep, indexed by ordinal from flat (ordinal 0) to steep (ordinal 12). With the offset sweep (11 candidates), the drag grid (16), and an angle $\times$ launch-power sweep (20), it is one of four candidate inventories over the same 15 N1 source members (Appendices~\ref{sec:app:secondparam}--\ref{sec:app:launchpower}), three realized as launch angles at saturated speed (pull radius saturates at 18.49~px) and one varying launch power inside that limit. A frozen ranker predicts a cost for every candidate and selects the lowest, breaking ties toward the lower ordinal. Each candidate's engine-truth verdict is read from the engine's own removal events, and a state belongs to the action ceiling when at least one candidate succeeds at first shot. The selection estimand is pooled top-1 over the three rankers on ceiling states against inventory-matched chance, a uniform draw over the scored inventory. Cell-unit and member-clustered AUC ($\mathrm{AUC}_m$) estimate ordering. The three frozen rankers share one architecture: a frozen CNN slot parser extracts object slots, and a depth-3 predictor advances a 236-dimensional latent carrier conditioned on the launch action and the requested $(\Delta, \alpha)$ pair. The hybrid model adds micro and macro predicate heads (1,837,690 parameters), and the pure-continuous models are widened to match (1,862,396). Each trained for 9,000 updates of batch 64 on 2,409 fitting lineages.

\subsection{The Estimand Taxonomy}
\label{sec:mth:estimands}

Three estimands separate what a comparison varies (Appendix~\ref{sec:app:estimands}): the training effect $\tau_{\mathrm{tr}}(\Delta)$ (hybrid against pure-continuous training, both executing $(\Delta, \mathrm{continuous})$), the symbolic-execution effect $\tau_{\mathrm{ex}}(\Delta, \alpha)$ (executed readout inside one hybrid checkpoint), and the adaptation effect $\tau_{\mathrm{ad}}$ (selection schedule against fixed-pair controls), which no reported contrast isolates. Condition labels are part of estimand identity, so zero-shot and few-shot contrasts are never pooled.

\section{The Pre-Registered Matched-System Protocol}
\label{sec:mth:protocol}

The scoring units, failure accounting, and disposition tokens were fixed by pre-registered plans before the model outcomes they score existed. The restatement statistics were not. The restatement's member unit, the ordinal success band, the disjoint-supports observation, and the choice of AUC as headline statistic were specified after the oracle-pass and completion-pass outcomes were seen. Top-1 was pre-specified as a reported outcome (0.5 margin on N1, descriptive on the offset sweep and drag grid, a frozen rule with a ticket-default stated prediction on the launch-power sweep), the chance reading is post hoc on N1, and $\mathrm{AUC}_m$ was the frozen decision statistic of the three added inventories. The launch-power controls were designed post hoc, and their speed-only baseline was seen before their freeze. Appendices~\ref{sec:app:protocol} and~\ref{sec:app:repro} record each protocol element and the validation commands.

Matched gameplay systems, extended with fixed-horizon, hybrid fixed-mode, and no-model ordinal-prior arms, score closed-loop behavior under the same contract. Every closed-loop claim is single-shot, decision-only, and scoped to the development N1 membership and the type010101 cross-split (Section~\ref{sec:syn:picture}, rows C16--C28). None is a gameplay-benefit, fresh-capture, or sealed-evaluation claim.
