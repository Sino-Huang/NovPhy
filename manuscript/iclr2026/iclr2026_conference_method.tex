\section{Setting and Formulation}
\label{sec:mth:setting}

An \emph{action-sparse persistent-effect environment} proceeds in discrete fixed steps. An agent acts at a few steps, and the physical state then evolves autonomously through collisions and rearrangement before settling. We instantiate this class on NovPhy \citep{pinto2024novphy}, which supplies the scenarios, generator families, and novelty levels. BG-NS-JEPA, the in-progress program studied here, supplies the models, the policies, and every number we report. Its scenario families serve as comparison strata: the original single-force and multiple-forces families, the rolling and sliding families added for breadth (N1), and matched appearance-novelty pairs (N2). One out-of-family replication uses CLEVRER \citep{yi2020clevrer} (Appendix~\ref{sec:app:clevrer:results}).

Each decision state receives a prediction request with a requested horizon $\Delta \in \{1, 5, 15\}$ (written $h$ later) and an output-description mode $\alpha \in \{\mathrm{continuous}, \mathrm{micro}, \mathrm{macro}\}$, which selects the supervised readout scored at that decision: the predictive latent, fine contact and support relations, or coarse predicates such as \texttt{steady-state}. All nine pairs run over one continuous latent carrier, and modes are readouts over it, never alternative state representations. If the scenario terminates $k < \Delta$ agent frames (carrier frames of 50 native steps) after the decision, scoring clamps the offset to $k$. A fixed-horizon ranker applies its $\Delta$-conditioned carrier $\Delta$ times per candidate, each advancing $\Delta$ carrier frames, so it looks $\Delta^2$ carrier frames ahead (1, 25, 225), while engine-truth outcomes resolve 97.8--400.9 carrier frames after the decision (Appendix~\ref{sec:app:lookahead}).

The closed-loop measurement scores selection. Each development N1 decision state carries a frozen inventory of 13 candidate launches, indexed by ordinal along a monotone launch-angle sweep from flat (ordinal 0) to steep (ordinal 12), and Appendix~\ref{sec:app:secondparam} scores two further inventories. A frozen ranker predicts a cost for every candidate and selects the lowest, breaking ties toward the lower ordinal. Each candidate's engine-truth verdict is read from the engine's own removal events, and a state belongs to the action ceiling when at least one candidate succeeds at first shot. Every measured N1 ceiling state has exactly one successful candidate. The three frozen model rankers share one small architecture: a frozen CNN slot parser maps observed frames to object centers, motion, and an availability mask, and a depth-3 predictor advances a 236-dimensional latent carrier conditioned on the launch action and the requested $(\Delta, \alpha)$ pair. The hybrid model adds supervised micro and macro predicate heads (1,837,690 parameters). The pure-continuous models are widened to match (1,862,396). Each trained for 9,000 updates of batch 64 on 2,409 fitting lineages (a frozen 2,400-entry pool plus N1 training lineages), cycling through all nine pairs.

\subsection{The Estimand Taxonomy}
\label{sec:mth:estimands}

Three estimands separate what a comparison varies (Appendix~\ref{sec:app:estimands}): the training effect $\tau_{\mathrm{tr}}(\Delta)$ (hybrid against pure-continuous training, both executing $(\Delta, \mathrm{continuous})$), the symbolic-execution effect $\tau_{\mathrm{ex}}(\Delta, \alpha)$ (executed readout inside one hybrid checkpoint), and the adaptation effect $\tau_{\mathrm{ad}}$ (selection schedule against fixed-pair controls), which no reported contrast isolates, so the adaptive controller never enters the engine-truth record. Condition labels are part of estimand identity, so zero-shot and few-shot contrasts are never pooled.

\section{The Pre-Registered Matched-System Protocol}
\label{sec:mth:protocol}

The scoring units, failure accounting, and disposition tokens were fixed by pre-registered plans before the model outcomes they score existed. The restatement statistics were not. The restatement's member unit, the ordinal success band, the disjoint-supports observation, and the choice of AUC as headline statistic were specified after the oracle-pass and completion-pass outcomes were seen. The protocol's elements are frozen plans re-hashed at launch, sealed membership, retained typed terminal failures, equal-update training with \texttt{training\_compute\_matched=false} disclosed, descriptive 95\% bootstrap intervals that support no superiority decision, right-censored end-of-window endpoints, reporting of every attempted cell, and a three-token disposition vocabulary (\texttt{supported}, \texttt{not\_supported\_by\_this\_experiment}, \texttt{readiness\_or\_precision\_insufficient}). Appendix~\ref{sec:app:protocol} records each element and its accounting, and Appendix~\ref{sec:app:repro} lists the validation commands.

Closed-loop behavior is scored under the same contract by matched gameplay systems, extended with fixed-horizon, hybrid fixed-mode, and no-model ordinal-prior arms, for which we claim no benefit. Every closed-loop claim is single-shot, decision-only, and scoped to the development N1 membership and the type010101 cross-split (Section~\ref{sec:syn:picture}, rows C16--C23). None is a gameplay-benefit, fresh-capture, or sealed-evaluation claim.
