% Paragraph 1 (#91-WP5 compressed): world models, the ranking use, the setting, the benchmark.
A world model approximates an environment's transition dynamics, conditioned on the current state and a candidate action. Dreamer learns behaviors from latent imagined rollouts \citep{hafner2020dreamer,hafner2025dreamerv3}, and TD-MPC plans with a learned latent model \citep{hansen2022tdmpc}. We study the planning use, in which the model ranks candidate actions, in an \emph{action-sparse persistent-effect environment}: one intervention sets off a cascade of collisions before settling. Our testbed is NovPhy \citep{pinto2024novphy}, a physical-reasoning benchmark \citep{bakhtin2019phyre,bear2021physion,yi2020clevrer}. Appendix~\ref{sec:app:related} discusses fixed-granularity designs.


% Paragraph 2 (#91-WP5 compressed from former ¶6-¶7): the granularity surface and the program split.
The program began with a granularity question. One action-conditioned model could select, per decision, a horizon $\Delta \in \{1, 5, 15\}$ and an output-description mode $\alpha \in \{\mathrm{continuous}, \mathrm{micro}, \mathrm{macro}\}$ (Section~\ref{sec:mth:setting}). BG-NS-JEPA is the in-progress program behind this $(\Delta, \alpha)$ surface, and no published NovPhy finding is its result. The paper's primary evidence is a closed-loop audit of its fixed-horizon rankers (Section~\ref{sec:syn:picture}).

% ===== Significance close (r7): "We observe" (post hoc; I49/I25) + candidate lesson (digest l.48 amended r7).
Closed-loop world-model evaluations such as World-in-World report task success without a per-state solvability ceiling \citep{zhang2026worldinworld}, and objective-mismatch work, including forward-predictor ranking of PHYRE candidates, shows that prediction quality need not track task success \citep{lambert2020objective,girdhar2020forward}. We observe, post hoc, a mismatch between two decision metrics under a measured per-state ceiling: ordering AUC moves with the inventory while pooled top-1 does not, in point estimate. A lesson we draw post hoc from the launch-power sweep, where the launch-power preference is also indistinguishable from one for in-support inputs: a high ordering AUC can come from a single-coordinate preference that a trivial baseline mostly matches while top-1 is zero, so report top-1 against a measured per-state ceiling, a coordinate-only baseline, and a within-coordinate AUC beside any ordering AUC, extending DOPE-style paired metrics and partial-input baselines \citep{fu2021benchmarks,poliak2018hypothesis}. % r4 I25: Girdhar arXiv:2006.10734 §5.1-5.2; lesson = digest l.48 (amended r7) without its outcome-channel tail (carried by spine (a)); r8 I25(3): DOPE ICLR 2021 §3.2, Poliak *SEM 2018; r8: in-support qualifier moved to the instance clause per Main; r8-followup: "partial-input" per lock §6
