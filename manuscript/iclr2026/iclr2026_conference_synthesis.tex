The ceiling serves as the denominator on each inventory. Engine replay finds a successful single-shot action for 7 of 14 measurable N1 source members on the original 13-candidate sweep, 9 of 15 on the offset sweep, and 14 of 15 on the drag grid. Against those denominators the three frozen rankers' pooled top-1 is $0/108$, $7/81$, and $8/126$, at or below inventory-matched chance ($0.0780$, $0.1030$, $0.0804$) in point estimate on each. Top-1 was pre-specified as a reported outcome (margin $0.5$ on N1, pre-declared descriptive on the other two inventories), and the chance reading is post hoc on N1. Top-1 failure alone is weak evidence at this size. A uniform top-1 draw per ceiling member misses every member with probability $0.567$ on the original sweep, $0.3732$ on the offset sweep, and $0.3074$ on the drag grid. We list these and do not combine them, because the inventories share members. % issue-92-selection-validity-v1/findings.md §2; summary.json chance_references.member_terms (k=1 for all 7 members); .local-artifacts/issue-93-second-parameterization-v1/comparisons.csv offset_ceiling, grid_ceiling, offset_top1, grid_top1, offset_miss_all, grid_miss_all
AUC is reported as a supporting ordering statistic. On N1 the descriptive cell-unit interval does not reach $0.5$ and the member-clustered one does, and we draw no conclusion from either. The inverted (maximum-cost) ranker, a post-hoc control, selects $15/612$ zero-shot and $21/612$ few-shot successes against chance $0.0407$, so these records give no support to a cost-sign reclassification. The restatement's member unit, the ordinal success band and disjoint supports on N1 (Figure~\ref{fig:app:bands}), and the earlier choice of AUC to headline N1 were specified after the oracle-pass and completion-pass outcomes were seen. % issue-92-selection-validity-v1/findings.md §3 (plan frozen after #87/#89); inverted: .local-artifacts/issue-91-inverted-ranker-v1/summary.json (per_condition); r6: cross-split AUC basis now in the Table 1 caption

Ordering and selection dissociate across the inventories, a post-hoc descriptive observation. The frozen reading matrix routed the second-parameterization result to Appendix~\ref{sec:app:secondparam}; we promote its per-inventory rows to the body. Member-clustered AUC, that replication's frozen decision statistic, is $0.5512$ on the offset sweep (\texttt{readiness\_or\_precision\_insufficient}) and $0.6178$ on the drag grid (\texttt{not\_supported\_by\_this\_experiment}), while pooled top-1 stays at $7/81$ and $8/126$. On the drag grid, predicted cost falls with pull radius (median Spearman $-0.7882$) and with drag\_y ($-0.8125$). The engine clamps pull radius above 18.49 px, so in the engine the grid is 16 launch angles at saturated speed, and 120 of 135 choices sit at one candidate. The statistics were pre-declared as descriptive, and this reading of them is post hoc. % .local-artifacts/issue-93-second-parameterization-v1/comparisons.csv offset_/grid_auc_member_clustered, grid_spearman_radius, grid_spearman_drag_y, *_disposition; summary.json arms.grid.chosen_ordinal_histogram; engine_action_check.json (18.491484, computed before freeze)

The hybrid ranker orders actions by the sweep coordinate. Its Spearman monotonicity with the launch sweep is $0.9986$, against $0.4924$ and $0.6130$ for the two continuous rankers ($0.8776$ pooled). Predicted-cost variance splits into $0.360$ between states, $0.004$ for the action main effect, and a $0.636$ residual. Ordinal~4 lands 3 of 7 N1 ceiling members (5 of 12 states), where every frozen system lands none, but 1/14 cross-split states, and ordinal~12 lands 3 of the 15 cross-split states where it is admissible but 0/12 on N1. % issue-92-selection-validity-v1/summary.json ordering_mechanism (spearman drag_x per_system, variance_decomposition incl. residual 0.63607); issue-92-cross-pool-audit-v1/findings.md constant-policy (a04 5/12 states = members 011, 012, 014)
State difficulty is left open. The probability that a non-ceiling state receives higher state-mean predicted cost than a ceiling state is $0.5758$ over 23 states and $0.5918$ member-deduplicated, and the member-clustered interval $[0.2653, 0.8958]$ covers $0.5$, so we make no claim about state-difficulty estimation in either direction. This analysis was specified post hoc after the oracle-pass and completion-pass outcomes were seen, and its intervals are descriptive. % .local-artifacts/issue-91-state-difficulty-auc-v1/findings.md (AUC orientation line 7; 0.575758, 0.591837, [0.265306, 0.895833]); r6: disclosure sentence compressed for budget

The primary rankers roll 1 ($h{=}1$) or 25 ($h{=}5$) carrier frames per candidate, while engine-truth outcomes resolve 97.8--400.9 carrier frames after the sealed decision frame (endpoint arms in Appendix~\ref{sec:app:lookahead}). At that lookahead, within a state, the frozen rankers' pooled top-1 choice is the engine-verified success no more often than an inventory-matched uniform draw on every launch-angle inventory tested, and in the engine every inventory tested is a set of launch angles at saturated speed.
