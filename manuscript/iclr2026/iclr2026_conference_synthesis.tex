Against the member ceilings of Table~\ref{tab:syn:oracle} ($7/14$, $9/15$, $14/15$, $8/15$ on the original sweep, offset sweep, drag grid and launch-power sweep), the three frozen rankers' pooled top-1 is $0/108$, $7/81$, $8/126$ and $0/72$, at or below inventory-matched chance ($0.0780$, $0.1030$, $0.0804$, $0.0521$), in point estimate on the offset sweep and grid. Top-1 was pre-specified as a reported outcome (margin $0.5$ on N1, pre-declared descriptive on the offset sweep and grid, pre-registered under frozen rule C24 on the launch-power sweep), and the chance reading is post hoc on N1. Top-1 failure alone is weak evidence at this size: the pre-registered criterion of a paired interval entirely below $0$ holds on the held-out split and, degenerately because top-1 is zero in every cell, on N1 and the launch-power sweep. A uniform draw per ceiling member would miss every member with probability $0.567$, $0.3732$, $0.3074$ and $0.6518$ (listed, not combined: the inventories share members). % issue-92-selection-validity-v1/findings.md §2; summary.json chance_references.member_terms (k=1 for all 7 members); .local-artifacts/issue-93-second-parameterization-v1/comparisons.csv offset_ceiling, grid_ceiling, offset_top1, grid_top1, offset_miss_all, grid_miss_all; .local-artifacts/issue-94-launch-power-v1/comparisons.csv ceiling, top1, top1_minus_chance, miss_all (0.6518), C24_disposition; N1 interval degenerate (0/108; lock v2 amended by Main r7)
AUC is a supporting ordering statistic: on N1 its cell-unit interval excludes $0.5$ and the member-clustered one covers it, and we draw no conclusion from either. The inverted (maximum-cost) ranker, a post-hoc control, selects $15/612$ zero-shot and $21/612$ few-shot successes against chance $0.0407$, giving no support to a cost-sign reclassification. The restatement's member unit, the N1 ordinal band and disjoint supports (Figure~\ref{fig:app:bands}) and the choice of AUC to headline N1 were specified after the oracle-pass and completion-pass outcomes were seen. % issue-92-selection-validity-v1/findings.md §3 (plan frozen after #87/#89); inverted: .local-artifacts/issue-91-inverted-ranker-v1/summary.json (per_condition); r6: cross-split AUC basis now in the Table 1 caption

Ordering and selection dissociate across the inventories, a post-hoc reading of pre-declared descriptive statistics. The frozen reading matrix routed the second-parameterization result to Appendix~\ref{sec:app:secondparam}, and we promote its rows to the body: member-clustered AUC, its frozen decision statistic, is $0.5512$ and $0.6178$ on the offset sweep and drag grid while pooled top-1 stays at $7/81$ and $8/126$. The engine saturates pull radius at 18.49 px, so the grid is 16 launch angles at saturated speed, and 120 of 135 grid choices sit at one candidate. On the launch-power sweep (Appendix~\ref{sec:app:launchpower}), AUC is $0.7441$ (\texttt{not\_supported\_by\_this\_experiment}) with top-1 at $0/72$: all 135 choices take the 80 px column at $50^\circ$ or $70^\circ$ and none succeeds, while the success sits at 17 px and $30^\circ$ or $50^\circ$ for 7 of 8 ceiling members. Controls we designed after seeing the sweep find the release time inert (median cost $\rho$ $1.0$), a speed-only baseline (seen before their freeze) at AUC $0.6889$ and within-power AUC at $0.5949$ $[0.4444, 0.7454]$ (\texttt{readiness\_or\_precision\_insufficient}), so most of that AUC is a launch-power preference.
