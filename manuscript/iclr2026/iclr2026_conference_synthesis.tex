\label{sec:syn:picture}

Table~\ref{tab:syn:oracle} is the primary closed-loop table: it gives the engine-truth ceiling, the frozen rankers' selection validity against that ceiling, and the controls that bound it. Figure~\ref{fig:teaser} shows the ordinal structure behind it, and Table~\ref{tab:syn:denominator} contrasts the denominator this measurement supplies with an assumed one. The proxy-scored anatomy of Section~\ref{sec:exp:anatomy} is heuristic, because its $t{=}600$ replay-cost proxy was construct-invalidated (Section~\ref{sec:exp:validity}).

The ceiling serves as the denominator of the result. Engine replay finds a successful single-shot action for 7 of 14 measurable N1 source members, with exactly one successful candidate per ceiling state. Against that denominator the three frozen model rankers select a success in none of 108 cells, and the no-model ordinal prior in none of its 36. Top-1 failure alone is weak evidence at this size: a uniform top-1 draw per ceiling member misses all seven members with probability about $0.567$. % issue-92-selection-validity-v1/findings.md §2; summary.json chance_references.member_terms (k=1 for all 7 members)
AUC is the headline statistic because its definition does not depend on where successes fall. The descriptive cell-unit AUC interval does not reach $0.5$ and the member-clustered one does, and we draw no conclusion from either. On the type010101 split both cell-unit AUC point estimates sit below $0.5$: $0.2039$ $[0.0762, 0.3324]$ for the frozen zero-shot arm, held out from that split, and $0.2732$ $[0.2182, 0.3239]$ for the few-shot arm adapted on it. Each covers the 216 of its condition's 612 cells on the 6 states with a success, clustered by state, so it carries less replication weight than the 612-cell top-1 counts. The inverted (maximum-cost) ranker selects $15/612 = 0.0245$ zero-shot and $21/612 = 0.0343$ few-shot successes against chance $0.0407$ (paired $-0.0162$ $[-0.0392, +0.0026]$ and $-0.0064$ $[-0.0270, +0.0118]$, 17 state clusters, descriptive), so these records give no support to a cost-sign reclassification. On N1 the chosen ordinals and the seven successful candidates do not overlap in any of the 207 scored model cells. The restatement's member unit, the ordinal success band, the disjoint-supports observation, and the choice of AUC as headline statistic were specified after the oracle-pass and completion-pass outcomes were seen. % issue-92-selection-validity-v1/findings.md §3 (plan frozen after #87/#89); issue-92-cross-pool-audit-v1/summary.json cross_split.per_condition.{zero-shot,few-shot}.auc.cell_unit (units 216, clusters 6, draws 10000; scored_cells 216; cells 612); inverted: .local-artifacts/issue-91-inverted-ranker-v1/summary.json (per_condition)

The hybrid ranker orders actions by the sweep coordinate. Its Spearman monotonicity with the launch sweep is $0.9986$, against $0.4924$ and $0.6130$ for the two continuous rankers ($0.8776$ pooled). Predicted-cost variance splits into $0.360$ between states, $0.004$ for the action main effect, and a $0.636$ residual. Ordinal~4 lands 3 of 7 N1 ceiling members (5 of 12 states), where every frozen system lands none, but 1/14 cross-split states, and ordinal~12 lands 3 of the 15 cross-split states where it is admissible but 0/12 on N1. % issue-92-selection-validity-v1/summary.json ordering_mechanism (spearman drag_x per_system, variance_decomposition incl. residual 0.63607); issue-92-cross-pool-audit-v1/findings.md constant-policy (a04 5/12 states = members 011, 012, 014)
State difficulty is left open. The probability that a non-ceiling state receives higher state-mean predicted cost than a ceiling state is $0.5758$ over 23 states and $0.5918$ member-deduplicated, and the member-clustered interval $[0.2653, 0.8958]$ covers $0.5$, so we make no claim about state-difficulty estimation in either direction. This state-difficulty analysis was specified post hoc after the oracle-pass and completion-pass outcomes were seen; all intervals are DESCRIPTIVE and no inferential claim follows from whether 0.5 is inside them. % .local-artifacts/issue-91-state-difficulty-auc-v1/findings.md (AUC orientation line 7; 0.575758, 0.591837, [0.265306, 0.895833]; disclosure sentence verbatim from its verdict section, ticket tokens #87/#89 replaced by stage names per r2 I11)

The primary rankers roll 1 ($h{=}1$) or 25 ($h{=}5$) carrier frames per candidate, while engine-truth outcomes resolve 97.8--400.9 agent frames after the sealed decision frame, a lookahead of between 0.25\% and 25.6\% of the outcome horizon (endpoint arms in Appendix~\ref{sec:app:lookahead}). At that lookahead, selection fails at within-state action discrimination along the candidate sweep. The zero-selection statement binds to the primary record of three frozen model rankers on the N1 membership (Table~\ref{tab:syn:oracle}). % .local-artifacts/issue-92-lookahead-control-v1/findings.md line 9; 0.25% and 25.6% DERIVED as 1/400.9 and 25/97.8 from findings.md lines 9 and 14
