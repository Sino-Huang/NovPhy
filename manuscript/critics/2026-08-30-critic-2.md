The brief instead says six rollouts have already been “collected and sealed,” while metric derivation and consumption are blocked pending authorization (lines 3, 29); it separately says that, after authorization, the common-compute comparator run still must occur (line 45). “Completion” could therefore be read either as an uncompleted sealed dataset or as an already completed final test. - **The comparator description is too compressed to preserve the predeclared comparison’s claim boundary.** “**fixed, single-axis, independent-axis, and parameter-matched factorized alternatives**” (TeX line 56) is not a reader-auditable account of the comparator set. Since the abstract makes the protocol its main substantive content, this loss of specificity makes the promised test less falsifiable to an OpenReview reader.
