## 0. Thesis in one paragraph

**Δbelief (Story Lock):** under a per-state ceiling measured in the engine, three frozen ~1.8M-parameter NovPhy rankers, pooled per inventory, pick the engine-verified success no more often than an inventory-matched uniform draw (in point estimate) on three candidate inventories over the same 15 N1 source members and on a held-out split, while their member-clustered ordering AUC runs from 0.4180 to 0.6178 with the inventory; for these rankers an ordering statistic does not certify selection. Ceilings (member unit, descriptive): 7/14 [0.2143, 0.7857] on the original 13-candidate sweep, 9/15 [0.3333, 0.8667] on the offset sweep, 14/15 [0.8000, 1.0000] on the drag grid; type010101 breadth pool 25/46 [0.3913, 0.6957]. Pooled top-1: 0/108 vs 0.0780 (paired −0.0778 [−0.0797, −0.0769]), 7/81 vs 0.1030 (−0.0166 [−0.0781, +0.0575]), 8/126 vs 0.0804 (−0.0169 [−0.0938, +0.1190]); held-out frozen zero-shot 13/612 vs 0.0407 (−0.0195 [−0.0368, −0.0045]); adapted few-shot 29/612 (+0.0067 [−0.0202, +0.0368]) bounds it, never pooled. Status: top-1 pre-specified as a reported outcome (#87 q2 margin 0.5 on N1; #93 pre-declared descriptive); chance reading post hoc on N1. Ordering: AUC_m 0.4180 [0.3135, 0.5252] on N1 (headline choice post hoc; cell-unit 0.4367), 0.5512 offset (`readiness_or_precision_insufficient`), 0.6178 grid (`not_supported_by_this_experiment`, primary arm). On the grid predicted cost falls with pull radius (median ρ −0.7882), which the engine clamps away above 18.49 px, and with drag_y (−0.8125); in the engine every inventory is a set of launch angles at saturated speed. Controls: 16 lookahead arms, second engine seed 26/26, exposure split, inverted ranker (post hoc), constant-policy exhibit. Lesson: verify the outcome channel and report top-1 against a measured per-state ceiling beside any ordering statistic. First-person B framing (+0.0887; 0.1299 vs 0.50) unchanged.
