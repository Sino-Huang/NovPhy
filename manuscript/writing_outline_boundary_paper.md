## 0. Thesis in one paragraph

In our NovPhy pipeline, an engine-truth closed-loop audit finds a successful single-shot action for 7 of 14 development N1 source members (member-unit, descriptive [0.2143, 0.7857]), with exactly one successful candidate per ceiling state (member counts at ordinals {2:1, 3:1, 4:3, 5:1, 6:1}). **AUC is the headline statistic**: the predicted-cost ordering of three small (~1.8M-parameter) frozen rankers places the successful action at or below chance on N1, AUC 0.4367 [0.4045, 0.4710] over 108 model cells and 0.4180 [0.3135, 0.5252] member-clustered. On the type010101 split both AUC point estimates sit below 0.5, per condition over 216 of 612 scored cells with a 6-member-cluster bootstrap: 0.2039 [0.0762, 0.3324] for the frozen zero-shot arm (held out from the split) and 0.2732 [0.2182, 0.3239] for the few-shot arm (adapted on it), never pooled. Inverting the ranking (max cost; post hoc) does not recover the successes: 15/612 zero-shot and 21/612 few-shot against chance 0.0407, paired intervals over 17 state clusters covering zero, so no support for a cost-sign reclassification. Every scored model cell chose a high-arc candidate (ordinals 7–12; no success in 70 candidate slots); the non-overlap of the two supports is a post-hoc N1 observation, not the headline. A fixed-horizon ranker looks Δ² agent frames ahead (1, 25, 225); the primary rankers roll 1 (h1) or 25 (h5) carrier frames against outcomes that resolve 97.8–400.9 agent frames after the decision (between 0.25% and 25.6% of the outcome horizon, derived), and at that lookahead selection fails at within-state action discrimination along the candidate sweep; a lookahead-limited failure is not excluded (the endpoint arms are stability-confounded). No claim about state-difficulty estimation in either direction (post-hoc state-level AUC 0.5758, member-clustered [0.2653, 0.8958] covers 0.5). The restatement (member unit, success band, disjoint supports, AUC as headline) and the state-difficulty analysis are disclosed as specified post hoc in §4/§6. The delta over objective-mismatch work is an engine-truth per-candidate AUC under a measured per-state ceiling in a single-shot cascade regime; the ceiling is the per-state solvability denominator that closed-loop world-model success reporting (World-in-World) omits, with PHYRE's solvability certification as precedent. The proxy-era boundary anatomy is a heuristic measurement-validity case study; its +0.0887 contrast is "unsupported: carried by one seed on an invalidated proxy".

- **First-person B only.** The proxy→engine reversal is motivation about OUR pipeline ("We initially read…"), never a claim about prior work. External-family results (#78, #79, #84, #88) bound our claims only.
