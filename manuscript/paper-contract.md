## 2. Writing decisions

- **Venue and stage.** ICLR 2027, discussion-phase revision of the same paper (upload deadline Nov 18 2026 AoE). Main text ≤ 10 pages (strict); appendix unlimited; AI-use section mandatory and uncounted. AC/reviewers may ignore changes "significantly different from the original": keep the problem, interface and model recognizably the same; change claims, evidence and presentation.
- **Reader.** World-model / JEPA / embodied-planning researcher who has not read NovPhy, our protocol, or our statistics conventions. Reviewers found the submission hard to follow: every term defined at first use, one notation table, captions self-contained with denominators.
- **Title (unchanged, verbatim).** "Granularity Is a Decision: Joint Horizon-Description Selection in World Models". The title names the variable the interface exposes, not a finding that adaptive selection is necessary or superior.
- **Thesis (verbatim, central to abstract, results, conclusion).** "A shared request-conditioned world model provides a useful interface for studying prediction granularity, while the present evidence does not show that learned joint selection outperforms the best fixed coarse request or direct task-specific prediction."
- **Genre.** Interface contribution + rigorous empirical study whose decision-level findings are mostly negative or qualified (m1 Q1 = A).
- **Contribution ranking (m1 D4 + R2-1 + R2-2, verbatim content).**
  - C1: One shared request-conditioned predictor serves all nine horizon–description requests; **at matched training updates per request**, sharing improves request-mean AUC over single-request specialists by +0.0848 [0.0742, 0.0952], although full-budget specialists can perform better.
  - C2: Prediction granularity materially changes rollout error, but its ordering depends on the training recipe and benchmark (see H3 for the mandated sentences).
  - C3: The same-checkpoint comparison measures J − J_cont = +0.0201 [0.0154, 0.0248] AUC; a qualified trained-policy contrast under at-most-budget evaluation, not an established practical improvement, not a causal demonstration of symbolic-feedback benefit, not superiority over the best fixed request.
  - C4: The expanded evaluation: 480 predictor-fit, 120 policy-fit and 400 evaluation NovPhy levels; 11,680 completed evaluation launches; explicit fixed-request and no-model controls; live execution; uncertainty and protocol qualifications.
  - The methodological contribution remains the joint request interface on one shared carrier, with description feedback and DP-distilled selection; per-step temporal adaptation itself is prior work (Neitz et al. 2018; Chatterjee & Khardon 2025).
- **Non-claims (m1 D5, verbatim intent; never write the opposite).** Scene-overlap rollouts are in-sample development evidence, not generalization. No learned-selector advantage over the strongest fixed coarse request. No general symbolic/macro decision benefit. No threefold compute saving (the corrected 0.784 MAC ratio is a cross-system comparison whose saving comes primarily from the narrower trunk). No equality claimed from an inconclusive contrast ("matches", "parity", "on par" are banned for inconclusive contrasts). No task improvement inferred from AUC. No cross-time-base transfer. The DP does not optimize recursive rollout cost. No added ranking value of rollout over direct success prediction. The NovPhy evaluation is identity-disjoint and within-distribution, not physical-novelty generalization. Live outcomes are descriptive and guard-vetoed.
- **Primary model (m1 D6).** The post-submission stack: repaired slot encoder + long-horizon and decision-contrastive predictor training, retrained on the 480-level predictor-fit split; fit and evaluation captures share the 50-native-step stride. Say plainly what changed since submission (one main-text paragraph, N3). The CLEVRER/Physion pixel experiments are separate studies with their own carriers, training and metrics; never imply they use the NovPhy checkpoint.
- **Macro (m1 D7).** Keep the macro target definitions each reported model was trained on. Report the observability audit as a limitation: redesigned temporal macro head F1 0.157 (parsed carriers), 0.113 (one-step predictions), 0.015 (open-loop rollouts) — audit-head measurements, not the primary model's macro head. Description labels identify supervised predicate-feedback modes, not semantic understanding.
- **Process boundary (m1 D10).** No new training, inference, engine evaluation, baseline scoring or statistical experiment. Existing records may be transcribed, plotted and packaged into anonymized supplementary files without changing metrics, populations or analysis. Uncompleted measurements remain explicit gaps; never placeholders, never promises of future completion. Building the manuscript is allowed.
- **Anonymity (m1 N10).** No repository URLs, usernames, machine paths (`/p/Project`, `/home/...`), GitHub issue links or author-only rebuttal tags in the PDF. Relative artifact paths and ticket numbers (e.g. "#114") in the reproduction/audit appendix are allowed.
