# Prospective causal event-development collection

This new development collection addresses the documented decision-observation
and action-support gaps. It does not rescue the failed angular pilot, search
new angles, or authorize training/fresh evaluation. The original-grid and
angular outcomes and all #70–#75 decisions remain unchanged.

## Population and fixed assignments

Use only the existing 350-lineage native development plan, with its original
XML, scenario specification/lineage, template, engine/generation seeds,
base-cluster identity, and exposure role. In each of the five existing normal
mechanics families, sort the 50 training identities and take zero-based ranks
`floor(i*49/19)` for `i=0..19`. Retain all ten calibration and all ten
model-selection lineages per family. This yields 100 training, 50 calibration,
and 50 model-selection lineages; no outcome or solvability filtering applies.
These development-check roles have already been opened and are not fresh data.

Assign each source one original (-80,10) reference and the unchanged twelve
angular actions from the completed pilot, in reference/ordinal order: radius
80 pixels, integer-rounded angles 5+7k degrees, k=0..11. Release 600 ms, tap 0.
All 2,600 assignments receive new rollout IDs, not new lineage identities.
Each assignment executes one shot from its reset level. This is supervised
first-decision action coverage, not evidence of complete multi-shot competence.
Old pilot captures are neither substituted for new assignments nor relabeled.

Save genuine agent RGB/time before candidate preparation/execution, using the
same candidate-independent readiness reference. Keep accepted-action history
separate from counterfactual candidates and retain the decision-to-capture gap.
Use the unchanged native player and physical step 0.0004 seconds, RGB stride
50, and maximum 30,000 capture steps/12 physical seconds/601 frames. Do not
extend old censored traces, modify game-clear rules, or synthesize endpoints.

## Resources and staged execution

Maximum eight isolated worker processes, separate ports/displays/work areas,
4 GiB RSS per worker tree and 32 GiB aggregate. Collection wall allowance is
172,800 seconds (48 hours), including smoke. Per attempt: 420 wall seconds,
180 shot-wait seconds. Retain at most 768 GiB of new artifacts and require
256 GiB free storage before starting another assignment. Zero capture retries
or replacement levels; record unattempted assignments after a resource stop.

The measured 65-assignment angular run used 6,964.526332 seconds and
12,422,927,674 bytes. Linear scaling gives roughly 77.4 serial worker-hours
and 496.9 GB for this inventory; ideal eight-way elapsed time is 9.7 hours,
not a speedup guarantee. Available resources at preparation were about 1.3 TiB
free disk, 49 GiB available RAM, and 16 logical CPUs. Stop on actual limits.
Accounting must include retained runtime copies, failures and interrupted
attempts, without repeatedly traversing the entire growing completed corpus.

Before rendering, implement/test/archive the source-bound isolated runner.
Then run exactly the first source's reference and first grid action in every
role/family cell: 30 assigned smoke captures, included in the 2,600 total,
within 3,600 seconds. All 30 must pass source/role/action/causality/RGB/trace and
resource integrity before full continuation. The smoke must exercise the
declared concurrent isolation. No gameplay success threshold selects smoke
continuation, membership, or actions. Missing/corrupt evidence stops execution.

Allow 43,200 CPU wall seconds for target/comparability validation and 14,400
CPU wall seconds for reviewable media publication. No model inference, GPU
scoring, or optimizer updates are authorized by this collection protocol.

## Targets and comparability

Record native clear, native level-fail, stable-without-clear stopping event,
or intact right-censoring; preserve exact observed intervals and terminal times.
Missing/corrupt records remain unavailable, not negative labels. A 12-second
capture deadline is not a 12-second deadline from the earlier decision image.
Early stability does not supply an unobserved later clear-by-deadline label.
Any later event-aware target must respect these distinctions and masks.

An individual supervised record uses its own genuine decision observation and
assigned action. It does not assert identical hidden reset state across other
actions. Separately compute the unchanged all-body/RGB/camera comparability
checks from the angular protocol. Any failed candidate makes the whole lineage
inadmissible for paired counterfactual ranking. Keep those records and failures
visible; do not relax tolerances or drop birds. Individually valid supervised
records and reset-comparable action groups are different evidence claims.
Neither supplies a live-policy superiority result.

## Data-readiness stop rule

After the complete inventory, require at least 90% valid captures within every
role/family cell and native-clear actions on at least ten independent training,
five calibration, and five model-selection lineages. Report every family and
all assigned outcomes, including no-clear and unusable groups. Failure yields
insufficient data readiness; no automatic cohort extension, replacement,
angle change, or optimizer sweep follows. Passing only supports preparation
of a separate numeric, source-bound symmetric hybrid/pure training protocol.

Calibration/model-selection records never enter optimizer updates. All learned
arms must eventually use identical permitted perception/history/action inputs;
engine labels are supervision/evaluation targets, not runtime hints. The pure
arm remains independently trained. Before any #76 fresh access, the full
adaptive-versus-pure, same-hybrid fixed-mode, strongest fixed/no-model,
nondegenerate useful gameplay, uncertainty, and full matched-compute gates
still require a prospective freeze and demonstrated readiness. This inventory
alone authorizes none of them.
