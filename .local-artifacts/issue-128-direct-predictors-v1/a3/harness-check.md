## Findings

- Phy-Q (primary definition): Bambirds 13.59, Eagle's Wing 12.11, Datalab 6.71, Pig Shooter -1.60, D-DDQN-Symbolic 6.42, D-DDQN-Image 8.76, Relational-Symbolic 8.40, Relational-Image 6.05; scaling factor 13.5754.
- sigma_human: the released sd_human_pass_rate column equals the population SD (ddof=0) over the 19 players of each player's mean average_rate in the scenario; the paper only says 'standard deviation of human pass rate'.
- Human data: 19 players released vs 20 stated in the paper; 10 task rows carry attempts = 100 (sentinel; average_rate 0).
- Heuristic per-template pass rate = mean over a task's plays of (LevelStatus == 'Pass'), averaged over the template's tasks; the scenario broad-gen value averages the paper's test templates (Supp. E).
- Bambirds was played on 0.229 of its tasks within 81-100 (the paper says heuristics used the learning agents' test 20%, i.e. tasks 81-100); 223 Bambirds tasks have 10 plays instead of 5.
- Pig Shooter = raw file Naive.csv (its test-template scenario means equal the 'Pig Shooter' CSV rows except 2_03, see below).
- Released heuristic scenario aggregates (the *_summarized_h_c.csv files and the Pig Shooter 2_03 cell of broad_generalization_all_agents.csv) pool tasks across templates; the paper says the scenario value averages the tested templates. The two differ only where a template has < 20 tasks (Naive, EagleWings, DataLab): Pig Shooter 2_03 broad gen 0.0100 (template mean) vs 0.0105 released (= pooled 0.0105).
- Pig Shooter local gen 0.10508 (template mean) / 0.10632 (pooled) vs Fig. 4b 0.1050: neither definition reproduces it, while the template mean reproduces Bambirds, Eagle's Wing and Datalab to 4 d.p.
- Human 1.86 attempts / 23.73 s per task (paper text): no candidate definition over the 19 released players reproduces them (human.attempts_candidates, human.think_time_candidates); [INFERENCE] they include the unreleased 20th player.
- Phy-Q with sigma_human ddof=1 gives scaling factor 13.947 (paper 13.58), so the published Phy-Q used the population SD.

## Native-task fidelity (within-template Relational DQN)

- templates: 75 templates (fifth_generation.zip: 75 dirs x 100 task XMLs); a within-template run trains and tests on ONE template
- train_test_split: Utils/Config.py update_level_index: tasks sorted by index, train = first int(0.8*100) = 80 (1..80), test = last 20 (81..100); paper Supp. D: 'first 80 ... last 20'
- num_update_steps: Parameters.py sets 20 (40 cross-template) but TrainLearningAgent.py line 219 overrides it to len(train)//5 = 16 for level_path != 'MattewLevel'; paper: N = training tasks per template / 5 = 16
- step_0_collection: level_per_agent=None and RepeatPlay(10) at step 0: every one of the 80 training tasks is played 10 times (800 episodes), split over the 10 workers (8 tasks each)
- steps_1_to_15_collection: each of the 10 workers plays 1 task sampled from its 8 (random.sample) once: 10 episodes per step
- evaluation: after training, each of the 20 test tasks is played once (RepeatPlay(1)) greedily (EPS_START=0); per-template metric = fraction of the 20 test tasks passed
- num_worker 10, num_level_per_agent 1 (source comment '#10'), training_attempts_per_level 1 (source comment '#10'), simulation_speed 100 (paper text: framework allows up to 50x)
- Published target: Relational-Symbolic 0.3220, Relational-Image 0.2393 (Fig. 4b row 'Local gen.' (Fig4_local_broad_gen.PNG); text: 'symbolic ... and image learning agents on average pass approximately 33% and 24%' (main.tex line 1454)); SD not reported (single run; no seeds or SD anywhere in the paper).
