## Decision P-other (2026-10-27): T6 readiness ceiling (C1-dino's R1 procedure on frozen V-JEPA 2 features) as a GPU job; CPU is infeasible

**Trigger.** #138 item 2, "readiness first (cheapest stop)". Before any predictor training, the arm needs the R1 endpoint readout's policy-split ceiling on frozen V-JEPA 2 features of true frames, computed exactly as C1-dino's R1 (#130 issuecomment-6383216316, stopped at 0.5948 < 0.65). The pre-registered rule is Decision P-adapter issuecomment-6436355731, item 6.

**Facts.**
- The procedure is C1-dino's. R1 = the #130 `PatchReadout` and `fit_readout`, imported unchanged (slot-query cross-attention over the 256 patch tokens, here at width 1408; 25 epochs, batch 128, AdamW 1e-3 / 1e-4, seed 20260908; 53 450 steps). It is fitted on the 273 692 fit frames at `rc.probe_positions` (13 919 shots, E99 targets from the #123 fit memmap). The ceiling is the pooled policy grid AUC (94 mixed cells, 1 504 candidates) of the tie-free cost of R1(true endpoint at min(225, end)) against R1(anchor), under `deterministic_scoring()`. The threshold is 0.65; below it, the typed stop is `readiness_ceiling_below_threshold`. Diagnostics with no gate: the E99 true-carrier ceiling (#130: 0.7223) and R1's pig presence / centre error against E99. Fit and policy splits only; the evaluation split is not read.
- Features: the frozen ViT-g AC encoder of `vjepa2-ac-vitg.pt` (Decision P-route issuecomment-6436321490), applied per frame exactly as the training cache will be (official `VideoTransform`, 2-frame tubelet, bf16 autocast, fp16 cache, `layer_norm` at load). G-port shows the features equal the official hub encoder + `forward_target` bit for bit on CPU (`gport/gport.json`).
- CPU cost (measured): 1.92 s per ViT-g frame on 4 threads, × 277 292 frames = 148 CPU-h for the features alone. That is beyond the ~10 CPU-h allowance, so the check runs as a GPU job.
- GPU estimate [INFERENCE]: features 1.44·10^17 FLOP ≈ 24–60 min + decode + ~200 GB of NVMe writes + hashing (job `138-features-readiness`, resumable, est. 75 min). R1 fit + ceiling (job `138-readiness`, not resumable, est. 180 min): 25 passes over 197 GB of features plus CPU-side layer norm; #130's R1 on its 2.4× smaller frames took 49 min. About 2–4 GPU-h in total on one exclusive GPU, with ~200 GB free on the local NVMe.

**Decision.** Run `scripts/gpu_window_issue_138_readiness.json` (`138-features-readiness` → `138-readiness`, one exclusive GPU; the orchestrator sets the GPU) in a night window, starting `138-readiness` at the beginning of a window. The result is `readiness/readiness.json` + `readiness/r1.pt`. Below 0.65, T6 stops with `readiness_ceiling_below_threshold`: no predictor training, and the CPU `--publish` writes the not-run child summary. At or above 0.65, Phase 0 continues; R1 is frozen and shared by every configuration and seed. Cap: 4 GPU-h, charged to #138. The rule and the threshold are not changed after the number is seen.

**Note for the orchestrator (sequencing).** The CPU projection (`projection.json`, [INFERENCE]) puts the child at 2 109–5 271 GPU-h against the ≈ 73 GPU-h remainder (draft `draft-decision-cap.md`). A `cap` decision costs 0 GPU-h and is therefore cheaper than this readiness job. The job is worth running only if the projection is to be revisited.
