## Decision P-route (2026-10-26): the `paper` branch (RNN) is the reference; T4 uses its low-dimensional transition model with the time-series recipe and the causal-uncertainty sampler

**Trigger.** #136 needs one reference implementation for the port, G-port and G-fid, and the ticket fixes the arm to "the paper's architecture for its non-video experiments", sampled with "the paper's causal-uncertainty schedule".

**Facts (vendored at `paper` @ dd65516, P-license above).**
- The README of both branches: `main` (475e0bc) is "our latest reimplementation with temporal attention", whose maze setting "is changed a bit" and whose checkpoints (maze2d medium/large, Minecraft) belong to that v1.5 code; `paper` holds "RNN code used by original paper for reproduction purpose". The paper's tables (Table 1 maze planning, Table 2 time series) come from the `paper` branch.
- On `paper`, every non-video experiment uses `DiffusionTransitionModel` with `TransitionMlp` (1-d observations): [z_{t−1}, x_t^{k_t}, emb(k_t), emb(c_t)] → residual MLP → stacked `GRUCell`s (the latent z_t) → linear head; training draws an independent noise level k_t per sequence element (`torch.randint` per time step, `DiffusionTransitionModel.forward`) and feeds the noisy x_t forward through the RNN; sampling (`DiffusionForcingBase.validation_step`) runs the context frames at noise level 0, then denoises the prediction horizon with the "pyramid" schedule (row m, step t at DDIM index m − t·uncertainty_scale, clipped): the causal-uncertainty schedule of Sec. 3.3.
- Two non-video recipes exist: **time series** (`df_prediction.yaml` + `exp_prediction.yaml`; App. D.9 / E.1: "1 mlp and 4 grus", z 1024, network size 64, v-prediction, cosine β, 1000 noise levels, 50 DDIM steps (η 1), AdamW lr 3e-4 / wd 1e-3, 2000 warm-up steps, gradient clip 10, batch 32, early stopping on validation CRPS-sum (patience 6 epochs of 100 batches, ≤ 200 000 steps, best checkpoint); "the exact same architecture and hyperparameters for all time series datasets", 8- to 2000-dimensional) and **maze planning** (`df_planning.yaml` + `exp_planning.yaml`: 4 MLP layers + 1 GRU, z 128, network size 16/32, x0-prediction, linear β, DDIM η 0, 10-frame stacking, no external condition, batch 4096 in the config / 2048 in the paper, reward-guided MCG sampling).

**Options.** (a) `main` (temporal attention): not the paper's architecture; rejected. (b) `paper`, planning recipe as primary: tuned to 2–4-d maze states with 10-frame stacking (our 46 carrier frames are not divisible by 10), no condition input, guidance-centred. (c) **`paper`, time-series recipe as primary**: a context-conditioned forecaster with exogenous covariates (our task: the anchor carrier is the context, the launch the exogenous input), dimension-agnostic by the paper's own use (our carrier is 236-d), and its published numbers (Table 2) test exactly this model for G-fid.

**Choice.** (c), the reference is `paper` @ dd65516:
1. **Model**: vendored `DiffusionTransitionModel` / `TransitionMlp`, imported unchanged; x = the E99 carrier (236-d, normalised by fit-split per-feature mean / std as the time-series loader does), external condition = launch5 (5-d) entering at the transition out of t0 (the official condition slot that conditions x_1; zeros = no-op after), 46 frames (0, 5, …, 225), context = frame 0.
2. **Training**: the official `DiffusionForcingBase.training_step` / `configure_optimizers` / `optimizer_step` semantics (per-element independent noise levels, constant SNR weight, AdamW, the manual warm-up applied after each step, Lightning's gradient-norm clip), re-implemented without Lightning in `world_model/baselines/diffusion_forcing/novphy.py`.
3. **Sampling**: the official prediction loop with the causal-uncertainty pyramid over the whole 45-step horizon (`chunk_size −1`, `uncertainty_scale 1`: 50 + 45 rows); the score uses the frame-225 carrier. Disclosure: the time-series runs in the paper sampled autoregressively (`chunk_size 1`, where the pyramid is degenerate); the planning runs used the full-horizon pyramid; the ticket fixes the causal-uncertainty schedule.
4. **Configurations** (≤ 3), native step count, batch and sample count: Decision P-adapter, posted before selection is implemented. **G-fid** target and band: Decision P-fid, posted before any fidelity run (a Table 2 time-series number is the candidate: same model and recipe, released data via GluonTS).
5. **G-port** runs the official `DiffusionForcingBase` under real Lightning (env `b127-dforce`, torch 2.13.0+cu130 = the novphy build) against the adapter on fixed CPU inputs: forward, per-step-noise loss, gradients, two optimizer steps (step 0 at the full LR, step 1 at warm-up LR 1/2000), one DDIM sampling step and the full causal-uncertainty sampling loop.

**Reason.** The paper branch is the code behind the published tables; the time-series recipe is the paper's single, dimension-agnostic hyperparameter set for conditioned low-dimensional sequence prediction, and it is the one with a cheap published number to reproduce.

## Decision P-fid (2026-10-26): G-fid = Table 2 time series, Exchange CRPS-sum (Tier T, official pipeline); band [0.0005, 0.0055) frozen before any run

**Trigger.** #136 G-fid: "the cheapest faithful reproduction of a published Diffusion Forcing number (released checkpoint → Tier E; otherwise a Tier-T retrain of the cheapest paper experiment), with a numeric band derived from the paper's reported quantity and frozen before the run".

**Facts.**
- Released checkpoints: the `paper` branch quickstart holds only the DMLab and Minecraft *video* checkpoints (qualitative rollouts; no table number, not the low-dimensional model); the `main` branch's maze checkpoints belong to the v1.5 temporal-attention reimplementation (not the paper's model). No Tier-E target exists for the paper's low-dimensional model.
- Tier-T candidates: Table 1 maze planning (d4rl + mujoco 2.1 stack, batch 2048–4096 × 120k steps, MCG-guided MPC evaluation) and **Table 2 time series** (same `TransitionMlp` model and the time-series recipe T4 uses; data via GluonTS; 32-sequence batches). Table 2 reports mean ± SD over five seeds: Exchange **0.003 ± 0.001**, Solar 0.289 ± 0.002, Electricity 0.023 ± 0.001, Traffic 0.040 ± 0.004, Taxi 0.075 ± 0.002, Wikipedia 0.085 ± 0.007.
- Exchange (`exchange_rate_nips`, 8 series, 6 071 business days, context = prediction = 30) is the smallest dataset; the recipe is identical across datasets (App. D.9 / E.1).
- Measured on CPU (4 threads, torch 2.13): one time-series update of the 7.4 M-parameter model on a 32 × 46 carrier batch takes 0.42 s; the official run validates every 100 updates (100 sampled paths × 5 windows) and stops after 6 epochs without improvement, at most 200 000 updates — up to ~50 CPU-h at 4 threads [INFERENCE: carrier-batch update rate plus the validation sampling], so a CPU run cannot be bounded by ~12 h in advance.

**Options.** (a) Maze planning (heavy stack, MCG evaluation, not the T4 recipe); (b) Solar / Electricity (tight relative spreads, larger data); (c) **Exchange** (cheapest, same model and recipe as T4).

**Choice.** (c), Tier T.
1. **Run**: the vendored official pipeline unchanged — `python vendor/main.py +name=gfid_exchange experiment=exp_prediction dataset=ts_exchange algorithm=df_prediction` (tasks `save_mean_std_metadata, training, test`: early stopping on validation CRPS-sum, test on the best checkpoint) — driven by `scripts/b127_dforce_fidelity.py` in env `b127-dforce`. Run-time settings that do not touch the method: `wandb.mode=disabled` (logger None), `lightning.seed_everything(20260908)` before `main` (upstream sets no seed), `experiment.training.checkpointing.save_last=true` (a `last.ckpt` at every validation, so the run resumes after SIGTERM → exit 75 via `load=last.ckpt`; RNG streams after a resume differ from an uninterrupted run), GluonTS' dataset directory pointed at `data/external/diffusion_forcing/` (MANIFEST.json). Precision: the official 16-mixed on a GPU; on a CPU Lightning has no fp16 mixed precision, so fp32 there (disclosed).
2. **Number**: the official test metric `test/crps_sum` (the run's printed test table), one seed (20260908).
3. **Band** (`fidelity/band.json`, frozen now: `sha256:00fc7bfcea677cecd9c2833755dad3a9f7254919597714a915218c0797384af7`): the paper's mean ± 2 SD at its reported precision, [0.001, 0.005]; our value is compared after rounding to the paper's three decimals, i.e. **pass iff 0.0005 ≤ CRPS-sum < 0.0055**.
4. **Where**: on a GPU through the night-window scheduler (`scripts/gpu_window_issue_136.json`, job `136-gfid`, prepared, not enqueued: the orchestrator decides with the timing probe and funding). The same resumable runner also runs on CPU (`--device cpu`, fp32) if the orchestrator prefers daytime CPU. Projection ≤ 2.5 GPU-h (≤ 200 000 updates; [INFERENCE] until the probe measures the update rate).
5. **Failure**: at most two logged repair attempts, each fixing a diagnosed port / environment defect, never the band; then T4 is dropped with `fidelity_not_reproduced`. Scoring (`--score`) refuses until `fidelity/gfid.json` passes.

**Reason.** Table 2 Exchange is the cheapest published number produced by exactly the model and recipe T4 uses; the band is the paper's own five-seed spread.

- CPU micro-benchmark (4 threads): time-series model 7 367 468 parameters, 0.42 s per update at batch 32 × 46 frames; planning model 12 034 060 parameters, ~1.1 s per update at batch 64 (compute-bound; batch 2048 ≈ 32×); one sampler call of 512 rows 27 ms (time series), so one level (16 candidates × 32 paths × 4 321 calls) ≈ 117 s on CPU.

**Choice.**
1. **Data** (fit split only, as #133/#134): `eb.FitShots()` (13 919 shots); each shot = the E99 carriers at frames 0, 5, …, 225 (46 frames; positions past the shot's end repeat its final carrier); the condition sequence holds the launch (`eb.launch5`) in slot 1 (the official slot that conditions the transition out of t0; `_preprocess_batch` zeroes slot 0) and zeros (no-op) after; nonterminals all 1. Batch b of a run = shots drawn uniformly with replacement from `eb.batch_rng(seed, b)`; noise levels and noise from the global torch generators seeded with the run seed (checkpointed). The policy split is used only for selection AUCs; the evaluation split only by `--score` after the readouts freeze and G-fid pass.
2. **Configurations** (selection: seed 20260908, full native budget, policy-split pooled grid AUC of the primary score, ties → fewer parameters, then declaration order; the selected run is reused as main seed 20260908; then seeds 20260909 / 20260910):
   - **`ts-native`** — the time-series recipe exactly (`df_prediction.yaml` + `exp_prediction.yaml`): z 1024, network size 64, 1 MLP + 4 GRU layers, v-prediction, cosine β over 1000 levels, 50 DDIM steps (η 1), AdamW 3e-4 / wd 1e-3 / betas (0.9, 0.999), manual warm-up 2000 (applied after each step), gradient-norm clip 10, **batch 32**, 16-mixed on CUDA; **early stopping** on validation CRPS-sum every 100 updates (16 fixed fit shots × 100 sampled paths, frames after the context; patience 6; **≤ 200 000 updates**), the best validation checkpoint is the model.
   - **`ts-100k`** — the same recipe for a fixed **100 000 updates**, final weights (the upper end of the paper's "usually converges between 50k to 100k steps"; it guards against an early stop that is premature on our data).
   - **`planning`** — the maze-planning recipe (`df_planning.yaml` + `exp_planning.yaml`): z 128, network size 32 (maze2d large), 4 MLP + 1 GRU layers, x0-prediction, linear β, DDIM η 0, AdamW 2e-4 / wd 1e-4, warm-up 100, no gradient clip, 16-mixed, **batch 2048** (paper App. D.10; the config file says 4096), **120 005 updates** (`exp_planning.yaml`), final weights; `frame_stack` 1 (46 frames are not divisible by 10) and the launch through the external-condition input (the maze recipe has none).
3. **Sampling and score**: the official prediction loop — context frame 0 at noise level 0, then the causal-uncertainty pyramid over the whole 45-step horizon (`uncertainty_scale` 1, `chunk_size` −1: 95 rows) — gives a sampled path; the frame-225 carrier → `harness.EndpointCosts` tie-free row; a candidate's row is the **mean over S = 32 paths**. Common random numbers: the k-th noise draw of a path is one fixed [32, 236] draw (CPU generator, seed 20261026) shared by every candidate and level, so a level's rows do not depend on what else is in the batch. Candidates are sampled in fixed chunks of 128 in a deterministic order. Any nonfinite sample → excluded typed row. S = 32: 3× the Monte-Carlo precision of 4 paths for ~0.3–1.6 GPU-s per level [INFERENCE; the timing probe measures it]; the paper's 100 paths serve its quantile CRPS, not an expectation.
4. **Guards / readouts**: G-rank (480 fit anchors × 16), G-action (`eb.action_sensitivity` on the per-candidate sample-mean frame-225 carriers), G-nonfinite, G-trained, the wrong-anchor control, the #127 global gates; position error descriptive (sample-mean carriers at frames 15/30/60/150/225); no G-collapse (not in the ticket's guard list; the score lives in carrier space); no native success head, no U + head control.
5. **Typed failures and resume**: a nonfinite loss ends a run (`nonfinite_loss`, `model.pt` with `complete False`, never retried); `ts-native` with no finite validation → `nonfinite_validation`. `resume.pt` (model, AdamW, GradScaler, warm-up step, torch generators, early-stopping state; `best.pt`) every 2000 updates and on SIGTERM → exit 75.
6. **Cap**: child cap 184 GPU-h (the upper end of the #127 coverage estimate, as #133 / #134 took theirs), 24 GPU-h per native seed, 48 h wall on 4 GPUs; funding follows the measured projection (P-coverage).

**Reason.** The two time-series configurations keep the paper's single low-dimensional recipe and differ only in the training-length rule (native early stopping vs the paper's stated convergence budget); the third is the paper's other non-video recipe.
