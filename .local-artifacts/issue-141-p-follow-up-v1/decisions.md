## Decision P-follow-up (2026-10-29): implementation details the pre-registration leaves open (frozen before selection)

Posted: https://github.com/Sino-Huang/NovPhy/issues/141#issuecomment-6480032792

**Trigger.** Implementing #141 (runner `scripts/run_issue_141_p_follow_up.py`, artefacts `.local-artifacts/issue-141-p-follow-up-v1/`). The ticket body is binding; this decision fixes only what it leaves open. No F1 / F1-D component has been trained; no evaluation content is used.

1. **Feature caching (U frozen).** For each U seed s, one pass of #129's `UnitRollout(s)` (Gate-B unit U, request F-15-macro, pair Δ = 15, 15 transitions from the anchor carrier to frame 225, exactly #129's U+head path) under `harness.deterministic_scoring()` writes `features/U-seed<s>.pt`: ẑ₂₂₅ of the 13 919 fit shots (level anchor, the shot's launch5), of the 480 fit anchors × 16 grid launches (G-rank, inherited G-action) and of every candidate of the 94 policy grid mixed cells, with the U checkpoint sha256 and per-row shot keys / ordinals. F1 seed s reads only this file for training and readouts. Evaluation and #110 features are never cached: `--score` / `--score-novelty` roll U from the sealed / novelty anchors inside the scoring process. Considered: rolling U inside every training step (identical values, 9 000× the rollouts) — rejected.
2. **Example layout.** A2 stores one observation per level and gathers it per shot; F1's observation is candidate-specific, so both arms hold one observation row per fit shot (row i = [z₀(level of i), ẑ₂₂₅ᵁˢ(i)] for F1, [z₀(level of i), z₀(level of i)] for F1-D; `task_indices = arange(13 919)`), with the launch4 and verdict of shot i. A2's `train_step` (scheduler step before optimizer step; BCE with logits), sampler (`train_indices_sampler`, 32 + 32 with replacement) and `make_optimizer` (Adam, cosine T_max 9 000) are used unchanged. Classifier = `A2Net(base_width 70, carrier_dim 472)`: 1 855 301 trainable parameters in both arms. Scoring: the cell's K candidates in one batch (eval mode); row value −logit (`eb.score_rows`, key `neg_score`).
3. **Seed pairing and paired streams.** F1 seed s reads U seed s (F1 20260908 ↔ U 20260908, etc.). For every seed and configuration, F1 and F1-D start from the same `torch.manual_seed(s)` initialisation (same architecture → bit-identical initial weights, checked) and batch b of both is `train_indices_sampler(verdicts, 64, eb.batch_rng(s, b))` (identical example indices, checked); they are trained as separate runs.
4. **Selection queues.** Six runs at seed 20260908 in declaration order (lr 3e-4, 1e-4, 1e-3): GPU 2 = F1 (after the U-20260908 feature pass), GPU 3 = F1-D; resumable (SIGTERM → `resume.pt` → exit 75). Then `--selection-readouts`: the policy pooled grid AUC (94 cells) of every run and `eb.select_configuration` per arm independently. The selected run is reused as seed 20260908; seeds 20260909/10 of both arms form the main training queue.
5. **U checkpoint identity and rollout replication (F1 guards `G_u_identity`, `G_rollout_replication`).** Identity: the Gate-B handoff checkpoint of U seed s (pinned in `eb.shared_inputs()`); its sha256 is recorded in each feature file and checked against the pin. Replication before the freeze (smoke; fit / policy data only): (a) `eb.action_sensitivity` of the cached 480 × 16 fit endpoints equals #129's `analysis/g-action-calibration.json` U value of the same seed within 1e-6 absolute; (b) #129's frozen U+head heads (hashes from #129's `readouts-manifest.json`) applied to the cached policy endpoints reproduce #129's U+head `policy_head_auc` of the same seed at 4 d.p. After the freeze (`--score`, the only step that reads U's Gate-B records): the `EndpointCosts` rows of the U endpoints F1 reads, on every mixed cell of every inventory, against the Gate-B U record of the same seed by `harness.endpoint_replication` (the program's replication rule: parsed quantities within 1e-3), every cell; the exact-equality share is reported. A failure fails the guard (F1's tokens become the stop token); nothing is re-run.
6. **Candidate/action alignment (`G_alignment`, both arms) and only-classifier updates (`G_classifier_only`, both arms).** Alignment: every feature row and scored row carries its shot key / ordinal; launch4 and launch5 of a row are built from the same action dict; scored rows follow the cell inventory order. Classifier-only: U is never in the optimizer (feature tensors carry no graph); the smoke checks that the optimizer's parameter set is exactly the classifier's, that U's state-dict hash is unchanged by a short training run, and every training report records the sha256 of the feature file before and after training (must be equal).
7. **Guard scope (as declared in the ticket).** Arm guards: `eb.arm_guard_block(["G_rank", "G_nonfinite", "G_trained"])` (G-rank on 480 fit anchors × 16 grid launches of −logit; G-nonfinite on the scored records) plus the extra guards of items 5–6. G-action is not a G_* entry of either arm: U's per-seed value on the cached fit endpoints and its comparison with 0.05 are reported in `extra.scope` (inherited, not relaxed). A2's G-port / G-fid (#128) are inherited and reported, not re-run (no new external code).
8. **Contrast guarding.** F1 − U: the arm block (global ∧ F1 guards). Within family: F1 − F1-D (global ∧ F1 ∧ F1-D), F1 − A1 and F1 − A2 (global ∧ F1 ∧ #128's published `guards_pass` of A1 / A2), F1 − U+head (global ∧ F1; U+head has no arm guards, as in #129's U+head − U). A1, A2 (#128 `records/`, key `neg_score`), U+head (#129 `records/`, key `head_neg_logit`) and NN-px / NN-car (#128 `references/`) are imported, never re-scored; each must match its child's `records-manifest.json` with a passing validation receipt (checked inside G_records). F1 − NN-px / NN-car: descriptive (no token).
9. **Novelty (descriptive).** `--score-novelty`: F1 and F1-D under the P-novelty rule (guard-passing arms, every seed); #129's frozen U+head re-scored on every seed exactly as #129's `cell_rows` (its frozen `head.pt` + `UnitRollout`; hashes from #129's readout manifest); NN-px / NN-car on the novelty anchors with #128's exact-launch rule (a launch no fit level executed, e.g. the mirrored level-5 grid, is unavailable: excluded and counted); table beside U / C* / P (`eb.novelty_reference_records`) and #128's A1, A2, A2-native, A4 novelty records (imported, hash-checked).
10. **Strata.** Cut points in the next decision.

**Disclosure.** While reading formats for this implementation I printed the field layout of one row each of the published #128 A2 and #129 U+head grid records and two entries of #128's NN-px `nearest` map (published records; field names and one value each). No F1 component existed and nothing is chosen on them.

## Decision P-follow-up (2026-10-29): distance-stratum cut points (frozen before training)

Posted: https://github.com/Sino-Huang/NovPhy/issues/141#issuecomment-6480033291

**Trigger.** Memorisation safeguard 2 needs the tertile cut points of the policy-to-fit pixel distance, frozen before training and computed from policy and fit data only.

**Options considered.** (a) All 120 policy anchors: cut points 0.3311 / 0.5050. (b) The 94 policy grid mixed cells: the selection population, which matches the 311 sealed grid mixed cells the strata are read on.

**Choice: (b).** A level's distance uses #128's NN-px metric: the mean absolute difference in grey levels (uint8, over 3 × 240 × 320) between the level's anchor frame 0 and the nearest of the 480 fit anchor frames (`run_issue_128_direct_predictors.pixel_orders`, exact integer sums). The cut points are `numpy.quantile(d, [1/3, 2/3])` (linear) over the 94 policy cells: **c₁ = 0.3246440972222222, c₂ = 0.4763107638888889** (the range of d is 0.0750–1.1117; computed on the CPU on 2026-10-29 before any F1 / F1-D training). Strata: near if d ≤ c₁, mid if c₁ < d ≤ c₂, far if d > c₂.

**Application.** `--score` (after the readout freeze) computes the same distance for each sealed evaluation level from its Gate-B anchor frame (`eb.read_evaluation_anchors`). It writes the distance and the stratum to `strata/evaluation-distances.json`, which is a records-manifest pattern. Strata are reported on the 311 grid mixed cells for F1, F1-D, U+head, A1, NN-px and NN-car, with U as context. Each stratum gets `eb.ranker_table` blocks (level-clustered bootstrap) and cell counts, with no tokens; no stratum is selected. The cut points are stored in `strata/cut-points.json` (written before the selection runs and pinned in `plan.json`).

## Decision P-follow-up (2026-10-29): compute accounting, feature files before the freeze, and queues

Posted: https://github.com/Sino-Huang/NovPhy/issues/141#issuecomment-6480065190

**Trigger.** The ticket fixes what is compared but not how F1's compute is counted or how the jobs are queued. Both are needed by the compute block (and the `eb.arm_block` budget safeguard), the smoke and the scheduler specs.

1. **F1's compute includes frozen U.** F1 is U plus a classifier, so for seed s its `training_gpu_seconds` = U seed s's training GPU-s (the #112 v3 retrain report that `eb.reference_compute` reads) + the U feature pass of seed s + the classifier's training. F1-D = the classifier only. `compute.json` reports the three terms separately. The budget safeguard reads the total, so if F1 − U is `supported` it fires by construction and the budget-qualified wording applies. MACs per candidate: F1 = U's 15 transitions + the classifier (`eb.macs_per_candidate` over both; breakdown reported); F1-D = the classifier. Wall per decision: F1 includes the U rollout. Considered: counting the classifier only for F1, which would understate F1's compute against U — rejected.
2. **Feature files are pinned before main training.** `--selection` (GPU 2) writes `features/U-seed20260908.pt`. `--smoke` writes the seed-20260909/10 files (fit / policy data only) and runs the replication checks of the previous decision on all three seeds. `--prepare` pins the three files and the U checkpoints; `--train` and `--readouts` refuse a feature file whose hash differs from the pin.
3. **Queues.** Selection: GPU 2 runs F1 (3 learning rates), GPU 3 runs F1-D, then `--selection-readouts` and `--smoke` run on GPU 2 (all `shares_gpu`; each job is minutes). Main (after the `--prepare` freeze commit): `--train` on GPU 2 (F1 seeds 20260909/10) and GPU 3 (F1-D), then `--readouts` on the same split (three arm-seeds each). `--freeze-readouts` runs on the CPU. `--score`, `--score-novelty` and `--validate` run on GPU 0 with `shares_gpu` false, after `137-publish-validate` and `140-score`. `--publish` runs on the CPU.
