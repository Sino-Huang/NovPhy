## Decision P-fid (2026-10-25): G-fid target = the paper's Fig. 4 pendulum value map, digitised; band frozen before any run

**Trigger.** #134 G-fid: "the paper's pendulum experiment (the released notebook): a numeric band derived from the paper's reported quantity and frozen before the run". The paper (arXiv 2010.14496v4) reports no pendulum number: Fig. 3 is qualitative, Fig. 5 (γ-MVE control) has no released code. The README says the notebook's last cell reproduces **Fig. 4** (γ = 0.99 value estimates vs value iteration on a discretised pendulum).

**Options.** (a) Digitise Fig. 3's pendulum densities (nine conditioning states, thin traces; fragile to resolution); (b) Fig. 5 returns (no released code; γ-MVE + SAC to re-implement); (c) **digitise Fig. 4 into one number**: how well the γ-model value map ranks states like the ground-truth map.

**Choice (c).**
- **Digitisation** (`scripts/b127_gamma_models_fidelity.py --digitise`, output `fidelity/digitised/fig4.json` + PNGs + maps npz). `pdfimages` on page 9 gives the three raster panels by PDF object id: Rewards (701), Ground truth (692), Value estimates (693), drawn unflipped (checked against a 600-dpi render), each spanning angle [0, 2π] × angular velocity [+8, −8]. Inverse magma colour map (nearest of 256 LUT colours; max residual 1.5/255). Check: the digitised Rewards panel vs the analytic `reward_fn` gives Spearman 0.9999.
- **Statistic.** Spearman ρ between a value map and the digitised Ground truth on the official `visualize_values` grid (`get_states(n)`), **n = 41** (the function's default; the notebook's last cell uses 20, reported descriptively). Our map: `V[c] = Σ_q prob_fn(c, q)·reward_fn(q)` (the `visualize_values` arithmetic) of the final model.
- **Paper's number.** ρ(paper Value estimates, Ground truth) = **0.7828** at n = 41 (0.7695 at n = 20, 0.8052 at n = 101). Myopic reference (value = one-step reward): ρ = 0.204.
- **Band [0.633, 1.0]** = [paper − 0.15, 1]. The paper is one unseeded run (no spread); 0.15 covers the resolution / digitisation sensitivity of the paper's own map (0.769–0.805) and run-to-run variation of a bootstrapped γ = 0.99 flow. The lower bound stays 0.43 above the myopic reference, so a model without long-horizon prediction cannot pass. Upper bound 1: agreeing with the ground truth better than the paper's figure is not a reproduction failure. Pass iff ρ(n = 41) is in the band. One run. At most two logged repairs, each fixing a diagnosed port defect, never the band; then T3 is dropped with `fidelity_not_reproduced`.
- **Run** = the Colab notebook (`gamma-pendulum.ipynb`, the README's version) cell by cell: `Args` as released (hidden 3 × 256, σ 0.1, batch 1024, Adam 1e-4 / wd 1e-5, τ 0.005, γ 0.99, sample discount 0.9, burn-in γ 0.5 for 2 000 steps, 50 000 steps), the released replay pool and SAC policy, env `b127-gamma_models` (torch 2.13 CPU float32, 4 threads). Deviations: device cpu; seeds fixed (torch 0, numpy 0; the notebook sets none); the in-loop plotting/video cell body skipped (display only; touches no parameter or RNG), the pre-training `visualize(prob_fn)` cell kept (it initialises the model's ActNorm); `save_model` replaced by a resumable checkpoint every 1 000 steps and on SIGTERM (exit 75); target shares the online prior after the deepcopy (torch 2.13 shim); the policy snapshot is loaded with `map_location`.
- **Cost.** CPU micro-benchmark 0.112 s/step (synthetic batches, 4 threads) → ~1.6 CPU-h training + ≤ 0.25 h evaluation [INFERENCE, `fidelity/projection.json`]; on a GPU 0.08–0.56 GPU-h [INFERENCE]. **CPU-feasible: it runs on CPU in daytime (4 threads, < 2 GB RAM), no GPU.**

**Frozen file.** `.local-artifacts/issue-134-gamma-models-v1/fidelity/band.json` (schema `issue_127_gfid_band_v1`), sha256 `38dfd8950e797d4375c6408566b22d7d1ec23a7a5b43c091520c4b7aa20343a5`. No fidelity run has started.

## Decision P-other (2026-10-25): T3 γ-model-car adapter, γ mapping, score, guards and the three pre-declared selection configurations

**Trigger.** #134 needs the NovPhy adapter (`world_model/baselines/gamma_models/novphy.py`) and runner (`scripts/run_issue_134_gamma_models.py`) fixed before any selection run. No selection run exists. None can run before the P-coverage funding decision (`funded.json`) and a G-fid pass.

**Options considered.** (i) Train on single frame-225 endpoints only. Rejected: a γ-model is a TD model of a transition chain. (ii) Standardise carriers before the flow. Rejected: the official code models raw states. (iii) Score by density (log p_γ at a goal carrier). Rejected: NovPhy has no goal state; the paper's own readout (Eq. 6) is an expectation over samples. (iv) Tune σ / sample discount / burn-in per γ. Rejected: these are not the arm's native axis.

**Decision.**
1. **Model = official defaults.** `gamma.flows.make_conditional_flow` over the 236-d E99 carrier conditioned on `{'s': carrier, 'a': eb.launch5}`: the Colab notebook's `hidden_dims [256, 256, 256]` and the function defaults (3 × [ActNorm, invertible 1×1 conv, conditional RQ-spline coupling], K 16, tail bound 10, logistic prior, no delta). That is **10 230 696 parameters**. The training step is the notebook's loop body: `BootstrapTarget` = (1 − γ)·N(s′, σ = 0.1) + γ·target(·|s′, a′); samples from the mixture with sample discount 0.9 (γ = 0.5 and sample discount 0.5 for the first 2 000 updates); MSE of model vs target log-densities; Adam 1e-4, weight decay 1e-5; τ = 0.005; batch 1024; **50 000 updates**; fp32 with torch defaults. The init is seeded on CPU, then moved to the device, so initial weights do not depend on the device. G-port (gport.json) is bit-exact (Δ = 0) against the official code at 4 arms (pendulum geometry + the 3 γ arms), and 3 injected defects are detected.
2. **Transitions.** Each fit shot gives carriers at frames 0, 5, …, 225 (46 states; positions past the end repeat the final carrier, as #131) and 46 transitions (s_k, a_k) → s_{k+1} for k = 0..45. `a_0` is the launch. Every later action and every next action a′ is the zero no-op, which is the policy after the launch. **The frame-225 carrier is absorbing** (s_46 := s_45), the same rule as the repeated final carrier. Total: 13 919 × 46 = 640 274 transitions. Each batch draws them uniformly with replacement (the official `ReplayPool.sample`) from `eb.batch_rng(seed, step)`. The global torch RNG (the official target sampling) is seeded with the run seed and checkpointed.
3. **γ mapping.** A sample from (s_0, a) is s_k with probability (1 − γ)γ^(k−1), i.e. weight (1 − γ)γ^k on transition k. The absorbing frame-225 carrier carries the tail γ^44. **Pre-declared configurations** (≤ 3; only γ changes): `g0.95` (γ = 0.95; horizon 1/(1−γ) = 20 transitions = 100 frames), `g0.978` (γ = 1 − 1/45; 45 transitions = the 225-frame cascade), `g0.99` (γ = 0.99, the official notebook's discount; 100 transitions = 500 frames). The frame-225 tail mass is 0.10 / 0.37 / 0.64 respectively.
4. **Score.** The expected tie-free cost (`harness.EndpointCosts`) over **S = 64 samples** x ~ p_γ(·|s_0, a). The samples come from the flow inverse of one fixed uniform draw [64, 236] (CPU generator seed 20261025, passed through the model's own prior transforms exactly as `TransformedDistribution.sample`). The same draw is shared by every candidate (common random numbers), so the score is deterministic and batch-invariant. **Nonfinite handling:** if any of a candidate's 64 samples is nonfinite, or its sampling raises the official inverse-spline assertion, the candidate becomes a retained typed row (`excluded`, costs None, `typed_failure` = `nonfinite_samples` / `sampling_assertion`). These rows count in G-nonfinite.
5. **Guards.** G-rank on the 480 fit anchors × 16 grid launches expected-cost matrix. **G-action = `eb.action_sensitivity` on the per-candidate sample-mean carrier** (the expected occupancy state), 480 × 16. G-nonfinite and G-trained as #127. **G-collapse is not applicable**: there is no latent, since the flow is a bijection of the carrier. Position error is not applicable: the occupancy has no timestep. The wrong-anchor control is run. There is no native success head, no U + head control and no matched variant.
6. **Selection.** Policy-split pooled grid AUC of the primary score under `deterministic_scoring()`, `eb.select_configuration` (4 d.p.; ties → fewer parameters → declaration order; a typed-failure run is never selected), seed 20260908, full budget. The selected run is reused as main seed 20260908, with seeds 20260909 / 20260910 at the selected γ. Training failures (`nonfinite_loss`, `sampling_assertion`) are typed and terminal, never retried.
7. **Gates and budget.** `--selection` / `--train` refuse without `funded.json`, a G-port pass and a G-fid pass. `--score` refuses without a G-fid pass. `--timing` (GPU probe, ≤ 0.5 GPU-h) needs its own posted decision before it is enqueued. Child cap 12 GPU-h (the coverage estimate 6–12). CPU-scaled projection: **3.1–29.1 GPU-h [INFERENCE]** (CPU 1.12 s/update × an assumed GPU/CPU ratio of 0.03–0.364; `projection.json`). The timing probe replaces it with a measured rate. If the measured projection exceeds the cap, P-cap applies before funding. The pendulum G-fid is CPU-only (0 GPU-h). Specs are prepared, not enqueued: `scripts/gpu_window_issue_134_timing.json`, `scripts/gpu_window_issue_134.json` (3 selection jobs, selection readouts, smoke), `scripts/gpu_window_issue_134_main.json` (after the freeze).
8. **P-novelty** (the protocol posted on #128–#131). After the primary scoring pass, T3 (if its guards pass) is scored on the 29 in-scope #110 cells' mixed novelty levels from the E99 v1 anchors through the same primary scoring path (`--score-novelty`). Descriptive only, no tokens, never in a disposition.

## Decision P-budget (2026-10-27): the smoke's cap check uses exclusive-equivalent device time, not shared-GPU process seconds

**Trigger.** The three selection runs (launched 18:00) share GPUs with other jobs (`shares_gpu`; GPU 0: 128-gfid-a4-fold2 + 137-select-native; GPU 1: 137-select-ar4). Their process seconds per update are 0.066 (g0.99), about 0.3 (g0.978) and about 0.5 (g0.95). The clean exclusive probe (`timing.json`, GPU 3) measured 0.061. The runner's smoke used process seconds for its cap check. With sharing, that projection is about 12 GPU-h (selection) + up to about 14 GPU-h (two seeds at a shared rate). That would fail `within_caps` (12 GPU-h) through contention alone. Under Decision P-budget on #127 (https://github.com/Sino-Huang/NovPhy/issues/127#issuecomment-6388410336), the program budget is device time, and per-process GPU-s on a shared GPU overstate it. The smoke has not run. No selection result has been read: this decision is outcome-blind and concerns accounting only.

**Decision.** `--smoke` (`smoke_projection`) records two bases.
- `process`: the recorded process GPU-s, as before.
- `exclusive_equivalent`: every update at `timing.json`'s clean exclusive rate (0.0607 s/update): 3 × 50 000 selection updates + 2 × 50 000 main updates, plus the declared readout (0.3 h) and score (0.5 h) estimates.

The cap check (12 GPU-h child, 24 GPU-h per seed, 48 h wall) uses `exclusive_equivalent` when a CUDA timing record exists, else `process`. The training queues come from the same basis. The ledger is unchanged: process GPU-s per run, counted once from each `report.json`. The publication still reports both. Test: `test_smoke_projection_uses_exclusive_equivalent_device_time`.
