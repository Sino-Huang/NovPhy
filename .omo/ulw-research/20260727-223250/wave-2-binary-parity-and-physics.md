# Wave 2: Binary Parity and Physics Surfaces

- The shipped `sciencebirdsgames/Linux.zip` contains `Assembly-CSharp.dll` and Unity's `Physics2DModule`. A static-string inspection of the assembly found `SymbolicGameState`, `AIBirdsConnection`, `ShootAndRecordGroundTruth`, `GroundTruthWithoutScreenshot`, `takeGroundTruthEveryNthFrames`, `ABGameObject`, `getRigidBody`, `LevelCleared`, and `IsLevelStable`. This proves symbols are compiled into the deployed binary, but is not a substitute for a live protocol smoke test.
- `ABGameObject` requires a `Collider2D` and `Rigidbody2D`, stores the Rigidbody, stores last velocity in `FixedUpdate`, exposes `getRigidBody`, and receives `OnCollisionEnter2D` with relative velocity and mass (`tasks/task_template_designer/Assets/Scripts/GameWorld/ABGameObject.cs:24-35,93-100,124-156,290-298`).
- Unity 2019.4 documents `Rigidbody2D.velocity` as linear velocity in units/second, `Rigidbody2D.mass`, `ContactPoint2D` collider pair/point/normal/relative velocity/impulses, and `Collider2D.GetContacts` as all non-trigger contact points. Primary sources: <https://docs.unity3d.com/2019.4/Documentation/ScriptReference/Rigidbody2D-velocity.html>, <https://docs.unity3d.com/2019.4/Documentation/ScriptReference/Rigidbody2D-mass.html>, <https://docs.unity3d.com/2019.4/Documentation/ScriptReference/ContactPoint2D.html>, <https://docs.unity3d.com/2019.4/Documentation/ScriptReference/Collider2D.GetContacts.html>.
- Macro-event anchors already exist: shot launch/batch start, collision callbacks, `Die`, TNT/explosion code, pig removal/level clear, bird exhaustion/level failure, and a stability value derived from Rigidbody velocities (`ABGameWorld.cs:378-395,665-720,752-789`; `ABGameObject.cs:102-122,124-156,258-287`; `AIBirdsConnection.cs:1253-1270`). They are not emitted as an event log.
